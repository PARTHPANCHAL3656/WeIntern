⚠️ The Problem

Context Switching Kills Productivity

Every time you switch AI tools, you lose hours rebuilding context from scratch. Your best thinking vanishes between platforms.

🔁

Repeating Yourself

Every new AI session starts with a blank slate. You spend 20 minutes re-explaining project goals before getting to the real work.

💨

Lost Knowledge

Critical insights, constraints, and decisions are buried in chat logs no one revisits. Valuable AI-generated context disappears forever.

🧩

Fragmented Workflows

Information gets siloed inside different AI tools. Your ChatGPT brainstorm can't talk to your Claude code session or Gemini research.

💡 The Solution

Capture Once. Reuse Everywhere.

Four steps is all it takes to turn fragmented AI conversations into a portable, searchable knowledge layer that follows you everywhere.

01

Capture Conversation

One click saves the full context, goals, and outcomes from any AI chat into a structured Capsule.

02

Generate Capsule

CapsuleHub distills the conversation into a reusable context block — searchable, versionable, shareable.

03

Store in Workspace

Organise Capsules by project, team, or tag. Your entire AI knowledge base in one clean sidebar.

04

Inject Into Any AI

Drag and drop any Capsule into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek. Instant full context transfer.
